## 1.PC -> Switch 
### **Devices**
- 1 PC
- 1 Switch (2960)
### **Connection**
- PC0 Fa0 -> Switch0 Fa0/1
- Use Copper Straight-Through cable.

### **PC configuration**

### **Test**
- Check PC configuration: ipconfig
- Check switch port: show interfaces status
- Check MAC address learned by switch: show mac address-table
- Generate traffic: ping 192.168.1.10
- <img width="600" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/377eb309-573b-4eaa-adc0-e6a6c4c7066c" />

### **Failure Cases**
- Cable disconnected -> Link goes down
- Wrong cable -> Cable cannot connect to the selected port
- Wrong IP configuration -> Communication fails
- Switch port disabled -> Port remains down
- <img width="600"  alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/0e2ff129-5be2-4114-8601-96355b9af3d5" />

### **Result**
PC successfully connects to the switch. Switch learns the PC's MAC address on Fa0/1.
